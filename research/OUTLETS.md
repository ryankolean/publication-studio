# Outlets

The research plan for SUMMIT-334: which outlets get a file in `publications/`
and in which batch. Every candidate below was opened and confirmed active
(a real wedding published within the last 12 months) on 2026-10-23, except
where the note says otherwise. A batch is one pull request.

Michigan and Florida are thin on purpose. Most regional titles found were
dormant, pay-to-publish, or a planner's own blog; they are listed under
"Considered and left out" so nobody researches them again. Florida weddings
do run often in the national and destination outlets.
